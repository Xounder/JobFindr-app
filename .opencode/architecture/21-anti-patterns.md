# Anti-Patterns

---

# Forbidden Patterns

- giant services;
- provider coupling;
- business logic in controllers;
- shared mutable state;
- frontend ranking logic;
- user data retention.

---

# Why

These patterns:
- reduce maintainability;
- increase coupling;
- reduce scalability;
- increase technical debt.
